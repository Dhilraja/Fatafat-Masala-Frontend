Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 01-01-2026

Last Updated: 01-01-2026

1. No Returns

All products sold are non-returnable once delivered.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are issued only if:

  • Wrong product delivered
  • Product received in damaged condition

Claims must be raised within 48 hours of delivery with order ID and photographic proof.

3. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

4. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled only before dispatch. Once shipped, cancellation is not permitted.
